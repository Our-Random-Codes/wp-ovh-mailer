# OVH Mailer

A WordPress plugin that sends all your site's emails through your OVH email account, ensuring reliable delivery via OVH's SMTP servers.

---

## What it does

By default, WordPress sends emails using the server's built-in mail function, which is often blocked or flagged as spam. OVH Mailer replaces that with your real OVH email account, so emails from your site — password resets, contact forms, order confirmations — actually reach the inbox.

---

## Setup

1. Install and activate the plugin.
2. Go to **Settings → OVH Mailer**.
3. Enter your OVH email address and password.
4. Select the port (leave it on **SMTP 465** if unsure).
5. Check **Activer l'envoi via OVH** and click **Enregistrer**.

That's it — your site will now send emails through OVH.

---

## Testing

Once configured, a test section appears at the bottom of the settings page. Enter any email address and click **Envoyer le test** to verify everything is working.
